Who we are

Moon Rentals, operated by Rey Luna, is a party and event rental business serving Savannah, Georgia and the surrounding area. These terms cover moonrentalsga.com, the quote forms we run on Facebook and Instagram, and the rentals themselves.

By requesting a quote or renting from us, you agree to what's on this page. If you don't agree with something here, tell us before you book and we'll sort it out.

Quotes and bookings

  • A quote is an estimate, not a reservation. Your date isn't held until we confirm it with you.
  • Prices depend on what you need, how many, the date, and where you are. We'll tell you the total before you commit.
  • We serve Savannah and roughly 30 miles around it. Farther out, ask us — we may still be able to help.
  • You need to be 18 or older to rent from us.

Delivery, setup and pick-up

  • We'll agree on a drop-off and pick-up window with you ahead of time. We do our best to hit it; weather and traffic occasionally have other ideas.
  • Someone should be able to let us in and show us where things go. If the site is locked, gated or blocked when we arrive, we may have to reschedule.
  • Tents need a surface we can safely anchor to. Tell us what you've got — grass, concrete, gravel — when you book, so we bring the right hardware.
  • Have the equipment stacked where we dropped it, and reasonably clean, when we come to collect it.

Taking care of the equipment

While it's at your event, the equipment is your responsibility. In practice that means:

  • Use it for what it's made for. Chairs are for sitting, tables are for tables.
  • Don't move a tent once it's set up, and take it down only with us.
  • Normal wear is expected and we don't charge for it. Damage beyond that, or items that don't come back, we'll invoice at replacement cost.
  • If the weather turns — high wind especially — get people out from under the tent. Safety first, equipment second.

Changes, cancellations and weather

Plans change. Tell us as early as you can and we'll work with you on rescheduling or adjusting the order. If severe weather makes a setup unsafe, we may need to cancel or change it — we'll call you, and we won't charge you for a setup we couldn't safely do.

Payment

We'll tell you the total, when it's due and how to pay before your event. We don't collect card numbers through this website, and we'll never ask you for one in a text message or a web form.

Text messages

Our text messaging program is optional. If you give us your mobile number and agree to texts — through the chat window on this site, or by asking us directly — here's what that means:

  • What we send: replies about your quote, date availability, delivery and pick-up times, and booking confirmations. No marketing blasts.
  • How often: only as needed for your request — typically a handful of messages around your event date.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply, depending on your carrier and plan.
  • Stopping: reply STOP to any message and we'll stop. Reply HELP for help, or call (912) 477-6959.
  • Delivery: mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We do not share or sell mobile information, or the fact that you opted in to text messages, to third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Text-message consent is never shared with anyone.

You can rent from us without ever agreeing to texts. Declining won't change the service you get or what you pay. How we handle the rest of your information is covered in our Privacy Policy.

Liability

Moon Rentals isn't responsible for injury, loss or damage arising from how the equipment is used once it's in your hands, except where that's caused by our own negligence. Nothing here limits any right you have under Georgia law that can't be waived.
