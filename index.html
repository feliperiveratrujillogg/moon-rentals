Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can — weekends fill up fast, especially in spring and fall. That said, send a request even if your event is soon; we'll let you know what's available.

Do you deliver and set up?

Yes. We offer delivery with setup and takedown, or drop-off only if you'd rather set up yourself. Tents are always set up by our team.

How do I pay?

Once we confirm availability and pricing, we'll walk you through payment options. A deposit may be required to hold your date.

What if it rains?

Our tent is a great backup plan. If you have questions about weather or need to adjust your order, call or text us and we'll work it out.

What areas do you serve?

Savannah, GA and the surrounding communities. If you're outside the immediate area, reach out — we may still be able to help.

Do you have other items besides tables, chairs, and tents?

We're expanding our inventory. If you need something specific, mention it in your request and we'll let you know if we can source it.
