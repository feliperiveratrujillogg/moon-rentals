Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

The short version: when you ask us for a quote, we collect your name, phone number, email and a few details about your event. We use that to get back to you and handle your rental. We don't sell it, we don't rent it, and we don't hand it to anyone who wants to market to you.

Who we are

Moon Rentals, operated by Rey Luna, is a party and event rental business serving Savannah, Georgia and the surrounding area. This policy covers moonrentalsga.com and the quote forms we run on Facebook and Instagram.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form. That's typically:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number and email address
  • Your event date and roughly how many guests you expect
  • Which items you need — chairs, tables, the tent, decoration
  • Anything else you choose to write in the notes

We do not run analytics, advertising pixels or cookies on this website. We don't track you across other sites, and we don't build a profile on you.

How we use it

To answer you and do the job. Specifically:

  • To send you pricing and check whether your date is available
  • To confirm details, schedule delivery and set up, and pick the items back up
  • To keep a record of the booking for our own business files

We will not add you to a marketing list or send you promotional texts or emails because you asked for a quote. If that ever changes, we will ask you first.

Who else sees it

We don't sell or rent your information to anyone, ever. A small number of companies handle it on our behalf, purely so the forms and email work:

  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) — if you fill out a quote form inside a Facebook or Instagram ad, Meta collects it and passes it to us. Meta's own handling of your data is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Formspree — delivers the request form on this website to our inbox.
  • Google (Gmail) — where our email arrives and is stored.
  • Vercel — hosts this website. Like any web host, it keeps standard server logs.

We may also share information if the law requires it — for example, a valid legal request from a court or government agency.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and booking records for as long as we need them to run the business and meet our tax and accounting obligations, and then we delete them. If you asked for a quote and never booked, you can ask us to delete your information at any time and we will.

Your choices

Write or text us and we'll take care of it. You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what information we have about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your information
  • Stop contacting you

We'll respond within a reasonable time, and we won't charge you or treat you differently for asking. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's privacy law — ask us and we'll honor them.

Security

We keep your information on password-protected accounts, and only Rey and the people who need it to do the job can see it. No system is perfectly secure, but we don't store payment card numbers, government ID numbers or anything else of that kind — we never ask for them through a form.

Children

This website and our forms are meant for adults booking an event. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and change the date at the top. If it's a significant change, we'll say so clearly rather than quietly editing it.
